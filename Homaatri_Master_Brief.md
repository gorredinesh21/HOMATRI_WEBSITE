## Instruction to Antigravity

Treat this document as the **product and positioning source of truth** unless a newer explicit decision from the founders overrides it.

Do not reinterpret Homaatri as:
- a generic SaaS product for home chefs;
- a social-media app;
- another Zomato/Swiggy clone;
- a simple listing marketplace;
- a pure delivery company;
- a generic restaurant-management system.

Homaatri is a **managed home-food / tiffin platform built around homemakers**.

The business has two equally important sides:

### Homemaker side
Homaatri helps a homemaker turn her cooking ability into a trusted, recognizable, sustainable food business.

### Customer side
Homaatri gives customers a trusted way to discover, subscribe/order, receive, review, and repeatedly consume home-cooked food.

### Homaatri's role in the middle
Homaatri provides and/or coordinates:
- branding and business support;
- content and social-media support;
- homemaker onboarding and verification;
- food/hygiene/service standards;
- menus and recurring ordering;
- payments/commerce;
- delivery;
- customer support;
- feedback and quality loops;
- operational infrastructure;
- eventually analytics, accounting, forecasting, and other business tools.

The social/content layer is **not the product's end goal**. It is one of the mechanisms used to build identity, trust, audience, and commerce around each homemaker.

# 9. THE TIFFIN MODEL — A CORE PART OF HOMAAtri

This is one of the most important strategic decisions to preserve.

## Homaatri is tiffin-first / recurring-meal-first.

The initial customer use case is:
- lunch;
- dinner;
- recurring meal plans;
- predictable meal schedules.

This is materially different from an on-demand food-delivery marketplace.

### Zomato/Swiggy-style mental model

> Discover → crave → order → individual delivery

This is optimized around:
- impulse;
- immediate demand;
- individual orders;
- unpredictable arrival times;
- fragmented delivery.

### Homaatri mental model

> Choose homemaker → plan/subscribe → recurring meals → planned preparation → grouped delivery → repeat

This is optimized around:
- routine;
- recurrence;
- predictable demand;
- planned production;
- route density;
- repeat relationships.

# 10. THE TIFFIN ADVANTAGE / OPERATING EFFICIENCY

The tiffin model can create several structural advantages.

## 10.1 Predictable demand

If a homemaker knows she has:
- 30 lunch customers;
- 20 dinner customers;

she has much better visibility into how much food to prepare.

Instead of:
> "I hope people order today."

the model can become:
> "I know approximately how many meals I need to prepare."

## 10.2 Lower potential food waste

Planned meal counts should reduce overproduction.

Mechanism:

> recurring orders → known quantity → planned production → lower overproduction → lower waste

Do not make an exact percentage claim until actual operating data exists.

## 10.3 Better delivery economics

Lunch and dinner create predictable delivery windows.

If many customers are geographically concentrated, Homaatri can potentially:
- batch pickups;
- consolidate deliveries;
- route multiple customers in one trip;
- improve rider utilization;
- lower delivery cost per meal as density increases.

This is one of the important potential operating moats.

## 10.4 Better planning

Recurring demand allows better:
- purchasing;
- production planning;
- packaging planning;
- delivery planning;
- staffing/assistance planning;
- menu planning.

## 10.5 Better repeat behavior

A customer does not need to rediscover food every day.

They can build a relationship with:
- one homemaker;
- one kitchen;
- one meal plan;
- one predictable lunch/dinner routine.

### Central tiffin thesis

> **Homaatri does not optimize for the fastest single delivery. Homaatri optimizes for predictable recurring meals.**

This sentence captures the operating difference very well.
